# Skyline Cadenza v26.3.0 Release Notes

Feature release that makes MTM scheduling co-isolation aware, adds DIA-NN + Carafe backfill and predicted peptide ranking, and adds forced peptides, a rank cap, and a per-slot target cap.

## New Features

### MTM co-isolation

- **Added a "Minimum target share of window current" slider** (under MTM constraints; default 1.5%, 0 = off). The scheduler no longer places a target in a window where co-isolated ions would take so much of the fill that it cannot be quantified. Every target in the slot is checked over its own elution. The competition is every identified precursor inside the emitted isolation band, targeted or not, and each one counts only for the part of its peak that co-elutes with the target. Both choices were measured on the 08/03 HeLa MTM acquisition. About 66% of the ion current inside the target windows came from precursors that were never targets, so a check that only looked at a slot's own targets would miss most of the competition. Counting a neighbour's whole peak on any overlap overstated the competition by more than 3x for 57% of targets. A rejected target falls through to its protein's next peptide, so the usual effect is a cleaner peptide for that protein rather than a lost protein. 1.5% is the break-even point on the acquired run: it removes 0.98 targets DIA-NN never re-identified for each good target it costs, at a cost of 1.7% of targets and 1.8% of protein groups. Predicted-only targets have no measured intensity and are exempt. Only identified precursors are counted, so the estimate is a lower bound on the true co-isolation.
- **The fragment clash check now covers co-isolated background**, not just a slot's own targets. Before, it only compared a joining target's top-4 fragments with the other targets in the slot, and solo windows were never checked. On the 08/03 HeLa run, 21% of targets had a top-4 fragment hit by a non-target precursor, against 8% hit by another target, and those targets were re-identified less often. A placement is now refused if any of a target's top-4 fragments picks up at least 1% of its own signal from co-eluting identified precursors inside the band. This is always on and has no control. Rescheduling the 08/03 GPF report was roughly neutral on breadth and raised the expected re-identification rate: Maximize Protein Coverage went from 88.1% to 88.9% and Balanced from 90.7% to 91.1%.
- **Added passengers**: precursors that already fall inside a scheduled window's isolation band and elute entirely within its firing time. The instrument fragments them either way, so listing them costs no cycle time and changes nothing in the exported Thermo method. They need at least 5% of the band's co-isolated current to be admitted. Against a full-FASTA search of the acquired 08/03 MTM run, 74.0% of emitted passengers were really re-identified, adding about 476 protein groups. The new **"Include passengers in Skyline push"** checkbox (off by default) chooses between a targets-only document and a targets-plus-passengers document. Passengers are tagged `passenger` in the transition list.
- **Added a "Max targets per slot" slider** (under MTM constraints; 0 = no cap, the default). It caps how many targeted precursors one MTM window can multiplex. A target that would join a full window goes to another window, opens its own, or falls through to its protein's next peptide. Untargeted co-isolated precursors and passengers do not count against the cap.

### Carafe predictions

- **Added a DIA-NN + Carafe backfill mode** (checkbox "Backfill unused schedule time with Carafe predictions"; needs both a DIA-NN report and a Carafe library). Confirmed DIA-NN targets are scheduled first. Carafe-predicted targets then fill whatever cycle budget is left, deduplicated against the confirmed targets by peptide and charge. Predictions are limited to the proteins DIA-NN observed plus any target protein list, so they add depth to confirmed proteins and cover proteins DIA-NN missed. The confirmed schedule is identical whether or not a Carafe library is loaded: predictions can only add targets, never move, drop, or reorder a confirmed one. Verified on a 32,431-precursor HeLa report with a 296,060-precursor Carafe library: 100% of the confirmed schedule was kept and about 4,400 predicted targets were added.
- **Added a "Carafe detectability predictions" input** (files or a folder of shards) with a **"Detectability cutoff" slider** (default 0.5). Precursors below the cutoff are dropped before scheduling; peptides with no prediction are kept. About 0.5 removes peptides that are ~90% unlikely to be observed while keeping most real detections.
- **Added a "Bromo peptide ranking" input** for ranking peptides within a protein in Carafe-only mode. When no Bromo file is loaded, Carafe detectability ranks the peptides instead. Either replaces the old fragment-intensity stand-in, which carried no detectability signal. The ranking is within-protein only and never affects protein order.
- **Added a "Predicted peak half-width (Carafe)" slider** (default 6 s), replacing the hardcoded 0.10 min synthesised peak. It applies only to Carafe-predicted precursors; DIA-NN and Skyline inputs use their measured peak boundaries.

### Target selection

- **Added forced peptides**: a list of peptides (text box or "Open peptide list...", one per line, with an optional charge) that is scheduled before anything else. Use it to carry the same validation peptides or intentional targets across schedules. Forced peptides obey every quality and instrument rule but ignore ranking, the rank cap, the per-protein bounds, and an exclusive target list. An entry with no charge tries its best charge state first and falls back to the others. The rest of the fill never drops a forced peptide. The status line lists entries that were not in the library or failed a rule.
- **Added a "Rank cap: top N per protein" slider** (under Peptide ranking; 0 = no cap, the default). Only each protein's top N precursors, by the selected peptide ranking, are eligible for scheduling. It counts precursors, so one peptide at two charge states uses two of the N. This is different from "Maximum peptides per protein": the rank cap shrinks the pool that scheduling chooses from, while the maximum limits how many of the eligible peptides are scheduled. In backfill mode the cap is applied separately to the confirmed and predicted peptides.

### Command-line tool

- `SkylineCadenza.Cli` gained a `schedule-diann REPORT.parquet PARAMS.json OUTPUT.json` command that runs the app's DIA-NN ingest before scheduling, for offline comparisons. The JSON input also accepts `forcedPeptides`, `peptideRankCap`, `minTargetIonShare`, `backgroundFragmentClash`, and `maxTargetsPerSlot`.

## Bug Fixes

- **Loading a Carafe library no longer changes the confirmed schedule.** In backfill mode the predicted peptides leaked into the confirmed schedule in several ways: protein grouping ran over DIA-NN and Carafe peptides together, predicted peptides changed each protein's ranking and window growth limits, a prediction could evict a confirmed target from a window, and turning backfill on switched the confirmed peptides' ranking to a predicted score they don't have. Loading Carafe also replaced the confirmed peptides' measured DIA-NN fragments with Carafe's predicted ones, even with backfill off. On a real HeLa report only about 32% of the confirmed schedule survived. All of these are fixed, and turning backfill on no longer overwrites your peptide ranking choice.
- **Scheduling is now reproducible across launches.** Peptides that tied on the ranking metric were ordered by the order the candidate list happened to arrive in, and for Carafe libraries that order changed on every launch. Identical inputs and settings could produce a different schedule each time (Jaccard similarity 0.58 to 0.74 between runs), so an exported target list and a Skyline document built from it could silently disagree. Ties are now broken by precursor ID and Carafe candidates load in a fixed order. Schedules with fully distinct ranking scores were already reproducible.
- **Fixed "No valid precursor m/z column found, line 1" when pushing a document that contains heavy-labeled standards** such as spiked PRTC. Heavy precursors are now left out of the transition list (they already reach the assay through the registered spectral library). The import also skips unexpected rows instead of discarding the whole assay. The status line reports how many heavy precursors were omitted.

## Breaking Changes

- **Removed the "Window packing" setting and its Legacy mode.** MTM windows are now always packed so that a window never grows across a co-eluting precursor that clashes with one of its members, and two windows firing at the same time never overlap in m/z. The old Legacy default allowed both, so windows could sweep over precursors they had just rejected and re-sample the same precursors at a fraction of the sensitivity. This reduces raw coverage: on the 08/03 HeLa GPF report, 9,079 targets and 5,525 proteins became 6,476 and 4,731 (-29% targets, -14% proteins). In exchange, the median target's share of its window's ion current rose from 3.30% to 8.48%, and the share of windows where a target holds under 1% of the fill fell from 12.9% to 6.7%. The CLI no longer reads a `packing` field; a request that still sets it is accepted and the field is ignored. Schedules made before this change can't be reproduced.
